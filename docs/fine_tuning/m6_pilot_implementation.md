**Hard guardrails** (unchanged thresholds, evaluated by the M4 metrics module): valid nonempty A/C/G/U fraction at least 0.99 of raw attempts; empty or unparseable fraction at most 0.01 of raw attempts; unique fraction at least 0.95 of valid outputs; exact TRAIN match rate at most 0.01 of valid outputs; largest duplicate share at most 0.01 of valid outputs. It makes the checkpoint ineligible for M7 and triggers no automatic rerun and no learning-rate fallback.

**Report-only diagnostics** (no threshold, nothing optimised against): nearest-TRAIN normalized edit distance (edit distance divided by the longer length), maximum edit similarity to TRAIN (`maximum_edit_similarity_to_train`, defined as one minus the minimum normalized Levenshtein distance over TRAIN, where the distance is divided by the longer sequence length; it is an edit-based similarity and is never called sequence identity), nucleotide and dinucleotide distributions with a total-variation distance to TRAIN, MFE, MFE per nucleotide, paired fraction from ViennaRNA, and length-stratified results, for D1 update 21 and for the accepted B1 pool side by side. **Consumption boundary.** Authorisation, repository, runtime, GPU, library, source-code, EncDec-asset, closed-training-evidence, schedule, and accepted-B1 checks all happen before consumption, and a failure there consumes nothing.
